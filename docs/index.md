Makes custom heatmaps and bubble plots for 1) looking at only specific
signaling pathways or cell types, and 2) pooling individual cellchat
objects. This is useful is cellchat was run on each patient separately,
and you want to look at the pooled pathway signaling across patients, or
to create a bubble plot of specific LR interactions, and incorporate how
many patients the interaction was identified in and the average
signaling strength.
